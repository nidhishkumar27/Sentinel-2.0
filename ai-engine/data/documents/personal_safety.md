# Personal Safety Emergency Guidance

## 1. Immediate Safety

- Move towards a well-lit, populated public location if safe.
- Avoid isolated streets and unfamiliar shortcuts.
- If someone is following or threatening you, do not confront them.
- Enter a staffed public facility such as a shop, hospital or police station.
- Contact local emergency services if you feel in immediate danger.
- In India, call 112.

## 2. Contact Trusted People

- Contact a trusted family member or friend.
- Share your current location through a trusted communication method.
- Explain the situation and your intended safe destination.
- Avoid sharing personal information with unknown individuals.

## 3. If Being Followed

- Avoid going directly to your home if you believe someone is following you.
- Move towards a populated, staffed location.
- Ask verified staff or police officers for assistance.
- Avoid accepting unsolicited rides or entering unfamiliar vehicles.

## 4. Information to Collect

- Current GPS coordinates and location accuracy.
- Whether the threat is ongoing.
- Description of the immediate safety concern.
- Nearby recognizable landmarks.
- Whether the user is alone.
- Whether trusted contacts are reachable.

## 5. Safety Restrictions

- Never encourage confrontation with a threatening person.
- Never invent the identity or intentions of another person.
- Never claim police have been contacted unless confirmed.
- Prioritize immediate safety and professional assistance.
