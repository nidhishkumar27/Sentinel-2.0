
# Flood Emergency Response Guidance

## 1. Immediate Flood Safety

- Move away from rising water and seek higher ground.
- Follow official evacuation instructions.
- Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater.
- Avoid bridges, drains, underpasses and fast-moving water.
- Stay away from fallen electrical wires and flooded electrical equipment.
- Call emergency services if trapped or in immediate danger.
- In India, call 112.

## 2. If Trapped by Floodwater

- Move to the highest safe accessible location.
- Signal for help from a visible safe position.
- Contact emergency services and share your location.
- Avoid entering floodwater to rescue another person.
- Wait for trained rescue personnel.

## 3. Electrical and Structural Hazards

- Do not touch electrical equipment while standing in water.
- Do not approach fallen power lines.
- Avoid damaged buildings and unstable structures.
- Do not enter flooded basements or enclosed spaces.

## 4. Drinking Water and Hygiene

- Do not drink untreated floodwater.
- Use sealed bottled water or officially confirmed safe drinking water.
- Avoid contact with contaminated water where possible.
- Wash hands with safe water and soap after floodwater exposure.
- Seek medical attention for injuries or suspected contaminated-water exposure.

## 5. Information to Collect

- Current GPS coordinates and location accuracy.
- Water level and whether it is rising.
- Number of people affected or trapped.
- Whether children, elderly people or people with disabilities are present.
- Nearby safe high-ground locations, if verified.
- Medical emergencies and immediate hazards.
- Availability of mobile connectivity.

## 6. Safety Restrictions

- Never recommend crossing floodwater.
- Never invent evacuation routes or safe shelters.
- Do not assume a road is passable.
- Never claim rescue teams have been dispatched without confirmation.
- Prioritize official evacuation instructions and trained rescuers.
