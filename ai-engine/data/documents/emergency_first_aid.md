\# Emergency First Aid Guidelines



\## Severe Bleeding

Apply firm, direct pressure to the wound using a clean cloth or sterile dressing.

Call emergency services for severe or uncontrolled bleeding.

Do not remove deeply embedded objects.



\## Burns

Cool the burn under cool running water for 20 minutes.

Do not apply ice, butter, toothpaste or other home remedies.

Seek emergency medical help for severe burns.



\## Unconscious Person

Check whether the person responds and is breathing normally.

Call emergency services immediately.

If the person is not breathing normally, begin CPR if trained

and follow the emergency dispatcher's instructions.



\## Road Accidents

Ensure the scene is safe before approaching.

Call emergency services.

Avoid moving an injured person unless there is immediate danger.

Do not remove a helmet from an injured motorcyclist unless

necessary to manage an immediate life-threatening airway emergency

and you are trained to do so.
