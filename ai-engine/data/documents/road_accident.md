
# Road Accident Emergency Response Guidance

## 1. Immediate Scene Safety

- Check for traffic, fire, fuel leaks and other hazards.
- Do not approach an unsafe accident scene.
- Warn approaching traffic only if it can be done safely.
- Call emergency services immediately. In India, call 112.
- Provide the exact location and number of injured people.

## 2. Severe Bleeding

- Apply firm, direct pressure using a clean cloth or sterile dressing.
- Maintain pressure until emergency responders arrive.
- Do not remove deeply embedded objects.
- If bleeding is life-threatening and a suitable commercial tourniquet
  is available, use it only if trained or following dispatcher guidance.

## 3. Suspected Head, Neck or Spinal Injury

- Avoid unnecessary movement of the injured person.
- Do not remove a motorcyclist's helmet unless necessary to manage
  an immediate life-threatening airway emergency and you are trained.
- Monitor breathing and responsiveness.
- Follow emergency dispatcher instructions.

## 4. Unconscious or Unresponsive Person

- Check responsiveness and normal breathing.
- Call emergency services immediately.
- If the person is not breathing normally, begin CPR if trained
  or follow dispatcher instructions.
- Do not give food, water or medication.

## 5. Information to Collect

- Accident location and nearby landmarks.
- Number of vehicles involved.
- Number of injured people.
- Visible injuries and bleeding.
- Whether anyone is unconscious.
- Fire, fuel leakage or traffic hazards.
- Whether emergency services have actually been contacted.

## 6. Safety Restrictions

- Do not move injured people unnecessarily.
- Do not remove embedded objects.
- Do not provide food or water to unconscious people.
- Never claim an ambulance has been dispatched without confirmation.
- Prioritize professional emergency assistance.
