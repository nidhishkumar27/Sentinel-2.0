
# Medical Emergency Response Guidance

## 1. Immediate Response

- Assess whether the surrounding environment is safe.
- Check whether the person responds when spoken to.
- Check whether the person is breathing normally.
- Call emergency medical services immediately for unconsciousness,
  difficulty breathing, chest pain, stroke symptoms or severe injury.
- In India, the emergency response number is 112.
- Follow instructions provided by the emergency dispatcher.

## 2. Unconscious Person

- Check responsiveness and normal breathing.
- If the person is not breathing normally, call emergency services
  and begin CPR if trained or follow dispatcher instructions.
- Ask someone nearby to locate an AED if available.
- If the person is breathing normally, maintain their airway.
- Do not give food, water or medication to an unconscious person.
- Avoid unnecessary movement if a spinal injury is suspected.

## 3. Severe Bleeding

- Apply firm, direct pressure using a clean cloth or sterile dressing.
- Maintain pressure until help arrives.
- Do not remove deeply embedded objects.
- If blood soaks through, follow emergency dispatcher instructions
  and maintain pressure.

## 4. Chest Pain and Stroke Symptoms

- Treat sudden severe chest pain as a medical emergency.
- Watch for sudden facial drooping, arm weakness or speech difficulty.
- Note when symptoms began, if known.
- Do not allow the person to drive themselves to hospital.
- Contact emergency services immediately.

## 5. Seizures

- Protect the person from nearby hazards.
- Do not restrain their movements.
- Do not place objects or fingers inside their mouth.
- Note how long the seizure lasts.
- Seek emergency assistance for prolonged seizures,
  repeated seizures or breathing difficulties.

## 6. Information to Collect

- Whether the person is conscious.
- Whether they are breathing normally.
- Visible injuries or symptoms.
- Approximate age group, if known.
- Number of people affected.
- Known allergies or medical conditions, if voluntarily provided.
- Time when symptoms started, if known.

## 7. Safety Restrictions

- Do not diagnose medical conditions.
- Do not recommend prescription medication.
- Never invent symptoms or medical history.
- Do not delay emergency services while collecting information.
- Prioritize professional medical assistance.
