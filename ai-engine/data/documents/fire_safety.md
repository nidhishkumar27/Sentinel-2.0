
# Fire Emergency Response Guidance

## 1. Immediate Response

- Raise the alarm and warn people nearby.
- Call local emergency services immediately. In India, call 112.
- Evacuate the building using safe, designated escape routes.
- Use stairs instead of elevators.
- Stay low if smoke is present.
- Never re-enter a burning building to retrieve belongings.
- Move to a safe assembly point away from the building.

## 2. Smoke and Toxic Gas Exposure

- Avoid breathing smoke.
- Cover the nose and mouth with cloth if necessary while escaping.
- Do not enter smoke-filled rooms or corridors.
- If trapped, close doors between yourself and the fire.
- Seal gaps around doors with available material if safe to do so.
- Signal for help from a window or another safe visible location.
- Follow instructions from firefighters and emergency responders.

## 3. Fire Extinguishers

- Only attempt to use an extinguisher if the fire is small,
  you are trained and have a clear escape route.
- Use an extinguisher appropriate for the type of fire.
- Never use water on electrical or flammable-liquid fires.
- Stop attempting to extinguish the fire if it spreads or produces
  heavy smoke.
- Evacuate immediately if conditions become unsafe.

## 4. Clothing Fires

- Stop, drop and roll if clothing catches fire.
- Cover the face while rolling if possible.
- Use a suitable fire blanket if available and safe.
- Seek medical assistance for burn injuries.

## 5. Burn Injuries

- Move away from the source of heat if safe.
- Cool the burn under cool running water for 20 minutes.
- Do not apply ice, toothpaste, oil or home remedies.
- Do not burst blisters or remove clothing stuck to the burn.
- Cover the burn loosely with a clean, non-fluffy dressing.
- Seek urgent medical assistance for serious burns.

## 6. Information to Collect

- Exact incident location and building type.
- Visible fire and smoke conditions.
- Number of people potentially trapped or affected.
- Known injuries or unconscious individuals.
- Available safe evacuation routes.
- Nearby hazards such as fuel, chemicals or electrical equipment.

## 7. Safety Restrictions

- Never encourage untrained people to enter burning buildings.
- Never advise using elevators during a fire evacuation.
- Never assume a building is safe because flames are not visible.
- Never claim firefighters have been dispatched unless confirmed.
- Prioritize evacuation and professional emergency assistance.
