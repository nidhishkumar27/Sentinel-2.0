
# Lost Person Safety Guidance

## 1. Immediate Safety

- Stay calm and avoid wandering into unfamiliar or isolated areas.
- Move to a well-lit, populated and staffed public location when safe.
- Suitable locations may include police stations, staffed transport terminals,
  hospitals, visitor information centres or established businesses.
- Avoid accepting unsolicited rides or following strangers to isolated places.
- If someone is threatening or following you, prioritize immediate safety
  and contact local emergency services.

## 2. Location and Navigation

- Check the current GPS location using the phone's trusted maps application.
- Identify the last known familiar location and nearby recognizable landmarks.
- Check the GPS accuracy and timestamp before relying on coordinates.
- Do not assume GPS coordinates guarantee the person's exact physical location.
- Prefer well-known public routes over shortcuts through isolated areas.
- If GPS or mobile connectivity is unavailable, ask staff at a safe public
  location for assistance.

## 3. Contacting Trusted People

- Contact a trusted family member or emergency contact.
- Share current location only through a trusted communication method.
- Explain the last known location, nearby landmarks and intended safe destination.
- If the phone battery is low, prioritize essential calls and location sharing.

## 4. Children and Vulnerable Individuals

- Children, elderly people and individuals with disabilities may need
  additional assistance.
- Encourage them to approach uniformed police officers or verified staff
  at staffed public facilities.
- Do not advise vulnerable individuals to travel alone through unfamiliar areas.
- If there is immediate danger or a medical emergency, contact emergency services.

## 5. Information Sentinel Should Collect

- Current GPS coordinates and location accuracy.
- Time of the last known location update.
- Nearest recognizable landmark.
- Whether the user is alone.
- Whether the user is a child, elderly or needs mobility assistance.
- Phone battery and network availability.
- Whether the user feels threatened or has a medical concern.

## 6. Important Safety Rules

- Never invent a nearby landmark, route, person's identity or location.
- Never claim that a contact has been notified unless confirmed by the backend.
- Never claim that police or emergency services have been contacted unless confirmed.
- Do not treat GPS coordinates as proof of identity.
- Prioritize safe public locations and trusted assistance.
